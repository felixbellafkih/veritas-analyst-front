Analysis

Target excerpt: قُرْآنًا عَرَبِيًّا غَيْرَ ذِي عِوَجٍ لَّعَلَّهُمْ يَتَّقُونَ

1. RESTORATION OF LITERARY MEANING (PHYSICAL AND BIOLOGICAL MECHANICS)

"A text assembled for active reading, formulated with explicit clarity, free from any possession of distortion, so that they may protect themselves from harm."

This verse describes the physical and linguistic structure of a body of instructions intended for human beings in the real world, according to a direct causal chain:

  1. Assembly for active reading (Q-R-A — Qur'anan) : This is not a detached sonic psalmody, but a set of grouped, structured statements actively read by the human eye and voice to extract concrete and applicable directives.

  2. Explicitation without opacity (A.-R-B — 'Arabiyyan) : Directly qualifying the preceding noun, this adjective defines the mechanical property of the statement: a clear, articulated formulation devoid of ambiguity. While the concrete idiom serves as an audible and written material vehicle, the structural load of the root denotes a transparent expression that delivers its meaning without loss or encryption to the listener or reader.

  3. Strict absence of deformation (GH-Y-R + dhī + A.-W-J — ghayra dhī 'iwajin) : In geometry and physical matter, 'iwaj refers to the curvature of a rod, the subsidence of terrain, or the deviation of a straight trajectory. Applied immediately after 'arabiyyan, the noun phrase attests that this explicit statement contains no internal anomaly, no logical contradiction, and no deviation likely to mislead the observer in their material actions.

  4. Active self-preservation (W-Q-Y Form VIII — la'allahum yattaqūn) : The declared purpose of this clarity and rectitude is strictly practical. Conjugated in the eighth verbal form (reflexive action that the subject performs on himself), yattaqūn refers to the act, for human individuals, of erecting a concrete protection around their physical, social, and behavioral integrity against the harms and destructions of reality.

2. AUDIT OF DEVIATIONS AND BREAKDOWN OF CONSENSUS

  1. The ethnolinguistic reduction of 'Arabiyyan and clerical monopoly: Classical exegesis (Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi) and treatises on legal foundations (Usul al-Fiqh, notably Ash-Shafi'i's Risala) exclusively reduce 'arabiyyan to the ethnic and linguistic affiliation of the tribes of Arabia, elevated to a privileged and untranslatable language. This reading served to disqualify the direct understanding of non-Arabic-speaking populations and to impose a caste of specialized interpreters. However, the root A.-R-B carries the mechanics of clear and distinct expression: the text precisely states that it is explicit by itself, which undermines any claim by a body of commentators to hold a monopoly on a meaning supposedly inaccessible to the ordinary reader.

  2. The frontal contradiction of the doctrine of abrogation (An-Naskh) with Ghayra dhī 'iwaj: While commenting on this verse by verbally affirming that the Quran contains no contradiction (thus Tabari glossing ghayra dhī 'iwaj as "without contradiction or divergence"), the legal and exegetical consensus simultaneously maintains the doctrine of Naskh (intra-Quranic abrogation), according to which dozens—even hundreds—of verses would have their legal status annulled and contradicted by other later verses, or even by external historical accounts (Hadith). To postulate that a text requires the internal annulment of its own statements to remain applicable amounts to de facto imputing to it a structural deformation ('iwaj) that verse 39:28 explicitly denies.

  3. The ritualization of Qur'an and the passive moralization of Yattaqūn: The majority tradition has dissociated the act of reading (qirā'a) from rational analysis by valuing mechanical vocal recitation to accumulate immaterial rewards, even without understanding a word of the text. In parallel, it has frozen yattaqūn in a posture of fearful emotion or passive ritual observance. The direct syntactic link of the verse demonstrates the opposite: it is not the recited sound that magically protects the individual, it is the understanding of a clear (A.-R-B) and undistorted (A.-W-J) statement that allows the human being to act concretely to protect themselves (W-Q-Y) from destructive errors.

3. LOGICAL AND TOPOLOGICAL CONCLUSION

Verse 39:28 establishes an equation of complete human autonomy: because the read collection (Q-R-A) is explicit (A.-R-B) and devoid of any internal distortion (GH-Y-R + A.-W-J), each individual possesses the direct capacity to use it to protect themselves (W-Q-Y) against the degradation of their living conditions and actions.

Topological coherence in Surah Az-Zumar (39): Surah 39 opens (39:1-3) with the requirement of an exclusive and unmixed orientation (Al-Din al-Khalis) towards the Central Authority, explicitly refuting the universal argument of all systems of human intermediation: "Those who take guardians besides Him say: 'We only serve them so that they may bring us closer'" (39:3). Verse 39:27 reminds us that all concrete examples have been presented to humans in this text, and verse 39:28 locks in the material proof: if the statement is entirely clear and without distortion, the existence of human intermediaries (clergy, spiritual masters, jurists interposing themselves between the text and the individual) loses all functional justification. Immediately after (39:29), the surah illustrates this law with the concrete example of a man torn between several conflicting partners versus a man under a single, coherent direction, before concluding (39:69-75) with the final evaluation where each individual is solely responsible for what they have accomplished based on this explicit text. In the contemporary world, this verse invalidates any blind delegation of critical judgment to traditional or digital institutions.
