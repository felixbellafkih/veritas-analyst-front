Analysis

1. RESTORATION OF LITERARY MEANING (PHYSICAL AND BIOLOGICAL MECHANICS)

The state of integrity (S-L-M) is not a social label, but the posture of an individual who systematically aligns their actions and decisions with objective and verifiable reality, without any prior allegiance.

When an individual affiliates with a closed group or a faction (a political party), they enter a structure of separation. The source text observes a recurring human mechanism: each faction eventually rejoices in its own dogmas and interests, isolating itself from others.

An individual in a state of integrity (S-L-M) can perfectly participate in the management of the city, propose optimization solutions, and collaborate with other humans. However, their adherence to a "party" can never be identity-based or unconditional. If the party's guideline (even perceived as "honest" at a given moment) contradicts objective reality or material justice, the individual must immediately reject the group's instruction. Maintaining partisan loyalty at the expense of facts constitutes an immediate breach of the state of integrity.

2. AUDIT OF DEVIATIONS AND BREAKDOWN OF CONSENSUS

Modern consensus and contemporary political ideology have generated two major deviations concerning this issue:

Firstly, the transformation of the "S-L-M" state into a sociological identity ("political Islam"). Groups create political parties by appropriating this label to capture an electoral base. This is a fundamental typing error: the state of integrity is a mechanism for individual information processing, not a campaign slogan or a tool for conquering power.

Secondly, the normalization of partisan spirit (Ta'assub). The source text explicitly condemns the fragmentation of society into sects and parties (Shiya'an and Ahzaban) where each group encloses itself in its own narrative (verse 30:32). Human political consensus accepts party discipline (voting according to the group's instruction rather than one's own conscience of the facts). The Quranic system rejects this delegation of responsibility. The individual is solely responsible for their information processing; delegating their discernment to a central committee or a party leader is a form of association (allocating absolute decision-making authority to a third-party source).

3. LOGICAL AND TOPOLOGICAL CONCLUSION

The action of organizing society, debating (Shura), and seeking material balance (Justice) is a requirement of the system. Collaborating with honest individuals to achieve this goal is valid.

However, the system's architecture prohibits identity-based confinement. The S-L-M agent is a free electron whose only compass is objective stability (H.-Q-Q). They cannot be a "party member" in the sense of an activist subject to a closed ideological line. From the moment group membership requires masking a factual data or validating an injustice to "protect the party," the individual must break this membership, under penalty of falling into occultation (K-F-R) and entropy (F-S-D). The honesty of a group is never a permanent guarantee; only continuous evaluation of facts maintains integrity.
