2. AUDIT OF DEVIATIONS AND BREAKDOWN OF CONSENSUS

Traditional consensus often interprets this verse as a scene of physical violence where angels strike the faces and backs of individuals at the moment of their death. This reading presents several deviations:

  1. Anthropomorphism of infrastructure administrators (Malā'ikah): The consensus depicts them as physical beings with arms and the ability to strike, which contradicts their nature as systemic agents operating in the Ghayb (the unseen) and whose actions are executions of protocols, not acts of human bodily violence.
  2. Reduction of the D.-R-B function (project a vector): The root D.-R-B is here understood as a physical "blow" or "strike." However, the function of this root is to apply a measure, project a vector, or impose a change of state. The idea of a violent physical impact is a semantic injection that ignores the systemic nature of the action.
  3. Literal interpretation of "faces and backs": Although "faces" (W-J-H) and "backs" (D-B-R) designate physical parts, the action applied to them is not physical aggression. The "face" is the visible interface, the "back" is the hidden part or the support. The application of measures to these two aspects signifies a complete and ineluctable modification of the individual's state, affecting both their external appearance and their internal functions.
  4. Reduction of W-F-Y (collect entirely): The term is often translated as "to cause to die," which is a simplification. It refers to a complete collection of an instance's state, a closure of its execution cycle without data loss.

3. LOGICAL AND TOPOLOGICAL CONCLUSION

This verse, located in a surah that addresses the confrontation between those who mask the truth and those who follow it, describes a critical transition phase for the Masked_Nodes (K-F-R). It is not a scene of physical torture, but a systemic description of the end of their execution cycle. The infrastructure administrators (Malā'ikah) proceed with an integral collection of their state (W-F-Y) and apply measures (D.-R-B) that exhaustively modify their visible interfaces (W-J-H) and their hidden aspects (D-B-R). This operation is an inevitable consequence of their misalignment with the truth, leading to a complete transformation of their state of existence. The surah warns against the consequences of such concealment, emphasizing that the system proceeds with a complete and inescapable reset for those who persist in masking data.
