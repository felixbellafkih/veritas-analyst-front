Analysis

1. RESTORATION OF LITERARY MEANING (PHYSICAL AND BIOLOGICAL MECHANICS)

The verse deals with individuals (men or women) who furtively appropriate the resources of others. The instruction is to break and interrupt their means of action and their levers of control (their "hands" in the functional sense). In physical reality, this translates into neutralizing their capacity to operate: seizing their tools, blocking their access, or restricting their movements (isolation or imprisonment). This neutralization is a strictly equivalent compensation for what they have illegally acquired, serving as an exemplary deterrent wall to protect society. The Central Authority guarantees this law through a perfectly calibrated and inviolable architecture.

2. AUDIT OF DEVIATIONS AND BREAKDOWN OF CONSENSUS

Traditional consensus (Fiqh and Tafsir) has interpreted this verse as the obligation to biologically and definitively amputate the physical hand of the thief.

This reading presents three major structural errors:

  1. Error in action typing (Q-T.-A. vs B-T-R): The text uses the root Q-T.-A., which denotes the breaking of a link or the interruption of access (disconnect), and not B-T-R, which denotes definitive amputation. Cutting the "hand" (the lever of action) means breaking the individual's operational capacity, not destroying their bodily integrity. As internal evidence, in verse 12:31, women "cut" (Q-T.-A.) their hands with knives out of surprise, without amputating them.
  2. Violation of the compensation equation (J-Z-Y): The root J-Z-Y requires a strictly equivalent and proportional return to the action (K-S-B). Irreversible mutilation for material theft (which can be reimbursed) breaks the mathematical parity of justice.
  3. Destruction of the repair cycle (S.-L-H.): The immediately adjacent verse (5:39) opens the way for repair: "Whoever repents after his injustice and repairs...". Biological amputation is irreversible and prevents the physical rehabilitation of the individual (to work and repay their debt). The system aims at reducing entropy, not the permanent destruction of an individual.

3. LOGICAL AND TOPOLOGICAL CONCLUSION

The Quranic penal system is not based on bodily mutilation, but on functional neutralization and strict compensation. By translating Q-T.-A. as physical amputation, tradition has transformed a security measure (restricting a thief's means of action) into irreversible destruction, thus violating balance and proportionality. In the contemporary world, "cutting the hand" of a thief (especially in financial, computer, or material crimes) corresponds exactly to freezing their accounts, revoking their access rights, or physically isolating them to stop their lever of action, while leaving them the physical possibility to repair their wrongs.
