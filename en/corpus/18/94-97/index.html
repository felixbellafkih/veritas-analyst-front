1. RESTORATION OF LITERARY MEANING (PHYSICAL AND BIOLOGICAL MECHANICS)

“They declared: ‘O possessor of the two synchronized cycles, indeed the active thermal thrusts (Ya'juj) and the reactive superheated masses (Ma'juj) generate massive material degradation on the earth’s crust. Can we assign you an extracted resource on condition that you establish between us and them a blocking barrier?’ He declared: ‘The solid anchoring in which the Supreme Ordainer has established me offers superior yield. So assist me with brute physical force, and I will establish between you and them a massive multi-layered dam. Bring me blocks of iron.’ Then, when he had symmetrically leveled the space between the two deviating rock walls, he declared: ‘Blow!’ Then, when he had brought the iron to a state of luminous incandescence, he declared: ‘Bring me so that I may pour a molten metal alloy entirely over it.’ Thereafter, they did not have the immediate capacity to overflow its upper surface, and they did not have the sustained capacity to pierce a passage through its mass.”

This sequence of four verses describes a complete geological and metallurgical engineering operation in four phases strictly observable in the physical world:

  1. Diagnosis of a thermodynamic runaway (18:94): The inhabitants expose a destructive physical anomaly (F-S-D / material entropy on the earth A-R-D.). The threat is not a single entity, but a binary process stemming from the same root of critical thermal friction (A-J-J):

    • Ya'juj (prefix Ya- — active action): the kinetic and geothermal thrust that generates friction and pressure.
    • Ma'juj (prefix Ma- — reactive container/mass): the rocky or magmatic material that absorbs this heat, melts, and propagates destruction.
    • The plural grammatical typing (mufsidun, isṭā'ū, istaṭā'ū): the text rejects the dual form, even though it uses it immediately after for the two rock walls (as-sadafayn). This grammatical choice encodes the physical nature of the phenomenon: a geothermal or magmatic surge is not composed of two solid objects, but of a chaotic multitude of flows, pressurized gas pockets, and incandescent fluxes.

  2. Technical arbitration and energy mobilization (18:94-95): The inhabitants propose an extracted material resource (KH-R-J) to build a simple frontal closure (S-D-D). The builder rejects the payment — his existing material establishment (M-K-N) being already optimal (KH-Y-R) — and redefines the structure: faced with a thermal flow, a simple barrier would yield. He demands collective physical force (Q-W-Y) in support (A.-W-N) to build a R-D-M: a compact, thick, and multi-layered embankment, capable of absorbing pressure and heat.

  3. Metallurgical process and thermal containment (18:96): The text details a heavy foundry and civil engineering protocol, step by step:

    • Mechanical framework (Z-B-R + H.-D-D): stacking of massive iron blocks, a very high-density material intended to fix an impassable physical limit.
    • Topographical leveling (S-W-Y Form III + S.-D-F in dual): the stack is precisely leveled from one bank to the other, synchronized between two opposing rock walls that naturally deflect flows towards this gorge (as-sadafayn).
    • Temperature rise (N-F-KH + N-W-R): forced air injection (blower) into the metal mass until the iron reaches incandescence (maximum light and thermal emission, nār from N-W-R), making the blocks suitable for welding and thermal bonding.
    • Hermetic sealing (F-R-GH Form IV + Q-T.-R): complete pouring (full emptying of crucibles) of a molten conductive metal with a lower melting point (copper or lead, qitr) onto the incandescent iron. By flowing into the interstices and covering the facade, the alloy creates a continuous, watertight, and perfectly smooth joint.

  4. Mechanical validation against the two vectors of rupture (18:97): A heavy, hot fluid blocked by a dam exerts exactly two physical stresses, encoded by two verbs and two distinct morphological loads on the root T.-W-A. (fluid, frictionless execution):

    • Failure of surface overflow (isṭā'ū + Z.-H-R): contracted verbal form (dropping of the letter ta), translating an immediate action with low additional load. The fluid mass naturally rises along the wall to overflow the visible crest (Z.-H-R), but the leveled geometry and the vitrified/smooth surface sealed by the molten alloy prevent any grip and any crossing.
    • Failure of deep penetration (istaṭā'ū + N-Q-B): full verbal form (complete presence of ta), translating a heavy and sustained energy mobilization. Unable to overflow, the thermal mass exerts continuous pressure at the base to melt the dam and drill a tunnel (N-Q-B) through it. It fails because the massive iron core (whose melting point is around 1538 °C, above most surface lavas) resists melting and mechanical thrust.

2. AUDIT OF DEVIATIONS AND BREAKDOWN OF CONSENSUS

  1. Mythological anthropomorphization (Gog and Magog): Traditional exegesis (Tafsir of Tabari, Ibn Kathir, Qurtubi) identifies Ya'juj and Ma'juj with two human tribes descended from Japheth, imprisoned behind a physical wall for millennia. This importation of external narratives generates an absolute material impasse: no human population of this magnitude can survive isolated without a visible ecosystem, and modern satellite imagery refutes the existence of such a demographic enclave. The root reading (A-J-J = overheating by friction) and the concordance with the only other occurrence in the corpus (21:96, where they are "opened" like a fault and "flow" N-S-L from every prominence of the ground H.-D-B) demonstrate that the text describes a thermodynamic and geological phenomenon, not an ethnic group.

  2. The warlike projection onto a thermal engineering process (S-D-D, R-D-M, H.-D-D, Q-T.-R): While some classical exegetes did note that radm designates a thicker and more compact structure than a simple sadd, the consensus nevertheless reduces the entire device to a military wall intended to block human infantry. However, pouring molten copper over white-hot iron blocks between two rock walls is an aberration for stopping soldiers (who can bypass the crests), but it constitutes exactly the specifications for a thermal shield and a watertight sealing dam against magmatic or pyroclastic flows.

  3. The anthropomorphic distortion of the verbs isṭā'ū / istaṭā'ū, Z.-H-R and N-Q-B: Classical rhetoric (notably Zamakhshari and Razi) correctly identified the morphological difference between the light form (isṭā'ū) and the heavy form (istaṭā'ū) as reflecting a gradation of effort. However, the Tafsir projected this distinction onto humans attempting to climb a smooth wall with bare hands and then dig through it with picks. This anthropomorphic bias obscures the physical precision of the verse: the light action corresponds to the natural ascent by hydrostatic/thermal thrust to overflow the surface (Z.-H-R), and the heavy action to the prolonged stress of melting and piercing (N-Q-B) against a massive iron core.

  4. The moralization of F-S-D and the fiscalization of KH-R-J: Translating mufsidun as "moral corruptors" and kharjan as "tribute/tax" projects a feudal political imaginary onto a material observation. F-S-D here measures the physical destruction and increase in entropy of a territory (A-R-D.), and KH-R-J simply refers to the provision of extracted material resources, which the engineer dismisses in favor of physical labor force (Q-W-Y).

3. LOGICAL AND TOPOLOGICAL CONCLUSION

Verses 18:94-97 expose a rigorous law of material engineering: a destructive thermodynamic anomaly (A-J-J generating F-S-D) is neutralized neither by financial transaction (KH-R-J), nor by a superficial partition (S-D-D), but by the mobilization of coordinated human energy (Q-W-Y) implementing material resistance (massive iron H.-D-D, heating to incandescence N-F-KH/N-W-R, sealing by molten alloy Q-T.-R) in a locked geometry (S-W-Y between S.-D-F). The final morphological variation (isṭā'ū versus istaṭā'ū) certifies the watertightness of the structure against the only two modes of physical failure of a dam: crest overflow and mass perforation.

Topological coherence in Surah Al-Kahf (18) and in the corpus: Surah 18 is entirely structured around the mastery of real laws in the face of deceptive appearances and material ephemerality (biological preservation in the cave, vulnerability of agricultural wealth, hidden structural causes in the episode of Moses). The narrative of the builder with the two synchronized cycles concludes this demonstration by showing that the stabilization of the physical world relies on the strict application of the laws of matter (causes and means), never on irrational intervention. In the immediately following verse (18:98), the text also specifies that this containment is temporary and will eventually be leveled when the global deadline arrives — directly linking to verse 21:96 where the generalized release of these thermal thrusts and masses from all terrestrial crests marks the final geological shift of the planetary infrastructure.
