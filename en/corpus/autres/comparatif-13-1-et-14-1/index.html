The point of origin allocates a flow that immediately enters a cycle of distribution or movement. Adjacent textual evidence (14:1): The text immediately following "A-L-R" describes an action of pure movement and extraction: "a scripture which We have sent down to you that you might bring mankind out of darknesses into the light". Verse 2 details "the heavens raised without visible pillars", verse 3 describes "the earth spread out, the firm mountains, the rivers", and verse 4 lists "neighboring plots of land, gardens, palm trees".

The difference is demonstrated by the very topology of the surahs: without the "M", the text deals with the trajectory of a flow. With the "M", the text first describes the architecture of the physical environment (the Matrix) that will support this flow.
