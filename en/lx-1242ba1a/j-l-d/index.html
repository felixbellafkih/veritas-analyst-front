Letter equation: J (Assembly) + L (Allocation) + D (Fixed_Vector) = Assembly allocating an impact on a fixed vector (the envelope).

Description

Assembled impact on a fixed vector. The assembly (J) allocates (L) a fixed vector (D) — application of an assembled force directed towards a fixed surface. The etymological link with جِلْد (skin/envelope) confirms that the targeted fixed vector is the outer envelope.
