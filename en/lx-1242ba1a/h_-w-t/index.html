Description

Mechanical action of applying a compression constraint (H.) on a continuity link (W) to keep it fixed to a terminal connection stop (T). This process forces the maintenance of a data load in a stationary residence state, preventing its propagation to the next cycle until the lock is lifted. 🚨 KERNEL SECURITY: This node strictly defines a register lock (Hold State) or a thread retention at an I/O breakpoint.
