Letter equation: N (Manifestation) + S (Flow) + K (Confinement) = Manifestation of a confined flow dedicated to a specific routine.

Description

Execution routine manifested in a closed framework and dedicated to a specific function. Refers to an encapsulated flow allocated to a precise and delimited task. No notion of sacrifice, termination, or destruction in the loads — only the manifestation of a flow in a dedicated container.
