ي م م

Logic function: DYNAMIC_MASS_AND_VECTORING

Letter equation: Y (Vector/Flow) + M-M (Double/Absolute Mass) = The existence of an encompassing mass (Yamm). In reflexive form (Tafa'ul): The action of applying this mass to oneself or covering oneself with it (Fabric, Sheet, etc...) (Tayammum).

Description

Fundamental state: A dynamic and encompassing material mass. Derived action: Application vector of a mass.
