Root H.-L-Q (ح ل ق) — Veritas lexicon (Maqasid al-Huruf)

Machine translation (Gemini) of the French Veritas lexicon.

ح ل ق

Logic function: SURFACE_STRIPPING // RING_TOPOLOGY

Letter equation: H. (Friction) + L (Allocation) + Q (Pressure) = Friction under pressure on an allocation, producing either surface stripping or circular topology.

Description

Friction under pressure applied to an allocation producing two mechanical effects depending on the context: (1) stripping of the superficial layer of a node or structure (STRIPPING), (2) formation of an annular topology by continuous circular friction (RING). No ritual notion in the charges — only the mechanics of friction-pressure on an allocated surface.
