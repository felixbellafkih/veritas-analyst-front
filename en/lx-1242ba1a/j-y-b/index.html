🚨 J-Y-B (Juyub in plural) refers to any anatomical fissure, slit, or hollow forming a cavity in the body structure. This term is not limited to the pectoral slit (cleavage). The target of the instruction (to cover the Juyub) is all bodily flaws exposed by morphology, variable according to posture and clothing.
