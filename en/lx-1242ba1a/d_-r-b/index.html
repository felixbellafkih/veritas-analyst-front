ض ر ب

Logic function: VECTOR_PROJECTION // STATE_MODIFICATION

Letter equation: D. (Mass) + R (Cycle/Routing) + B (Base/Interface) = Vector projection of a measurement (D+R) onto a base (B) operating a transfer or state modification.

Description

Action of imposing a measure, applying a parameter, or triggering a vector to cause a change of state. 🚨 KERNEL SECURITY: D.-R-B exclusively refers to the vectorial projection of a vector or the application of a measure inducing a change of state. Coupled with bi + R-J-L (the leg as a vector), it describes the deliberate lifting or extension of the lower limb: raising the leg, excessively lengthening the stride, or projecting it laterally. It is in no way a physical impact of the foot on the ground.
