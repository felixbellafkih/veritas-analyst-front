Logic function: SEPARATION_ASSEMBLY_LINK // REDUCED_ASSEMBLY

Letter equation: Z (Separation) + J (Assembly) + W (Link) = Separation reducing the assembly of the link.

Description

Reduced assembly. Separation (Z) reduces the assembly (J) of the bond (W) — the assembled bond is amputated of its substance.
