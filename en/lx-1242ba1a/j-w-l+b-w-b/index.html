Root J-W-L+B-W-B (ج و ل + ب و ب) — Veritas lexicon (Maqasid al-Huruf)

Machine translation (Gemini) of the French Veritas lexicon.

ج و ل + ب و ب

Logic function: CIRCULAR_WRAP // STRUCTURED_OPENING

Letter equation: J-W-L (Circulation/Revolution) + B-W-B (Structured Opening) = A circular wrapping of the body with an access opening — garment that rotates around and whose lower slit is the regulated variable.

Description

Compound word resulting from the contraction of J-W-L (revolution/circulation around an axis) and B-W-B (opening/structured access). Physically designates an enveloping garment that revolves around the body and whose structure includes an opening from the bottom or side. Form: fabric circulating around the body with a lower access slit.

Kernel directive

🚨 Traditional Tafsir interprets 'yudnīna min jalābibihinna' as 'to draw the veil over the face'. This reading reverses the vector of D-N-W (which is downward, not upward) and ignores the partitive 'min' (a part of). The correct reading: to lower the bottom hem of the circular garment to cover the lower anatomical fissures (J-Y-B) exposed during movement.
